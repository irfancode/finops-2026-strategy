#### Community Engagement

- Slack community building
- FinOps Foundation membership

### Phase 2: Product-Led Growth (Months 4-12)

#### Freemium Model
```
Tiers:
├── Free: Core features, 1 provider, 1K resources
├── Pro: All features, 3 providers, 10K resources ($99/mo)
└── Enterprise: Unlimited, SSO, SLA, dedicated ($499+/mo)

Conversion Funnel:
Free → Pro: 5% monthly
Pro → Enterprise: 2% monthly

#### Viral Loops
1. **Team invites** - Share dashboards with teammates
2. **Report sharing** - Exportable reports with watermark
3. **Integration sharing** - Connect Slack, Jira, etc.
4. **Community badges** - Gamification

### Phase 3: Sales-Led Growth (Months 10-24)

#### Enterprise Sales
```
Sales Playbook:
├── Inbound: Demo requests from website
├── Outbound: Targeted enterprise outreach
├── Partner: MSP and SI partnerships
└── Marketplace: AWS, Azure, GCP marketplaces

Sales Cycle:
- SMB: 2 weeks (self-serve)
